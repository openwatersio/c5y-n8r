# Data licensing and provenance

The MIT license covers this repository's code and documentation. Imported data and generated datasets retain the applicable upstream terms; the code license does not relicense them.

The current imported structured data in `sources/wikidata/`, `sources/taxonomy/`, and `sources/research/` is provided by Wikidata under [CC0 1.0](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing). `sources/manifest.json` preserves revision links, retrieval dates, and hashes. The generated binary derives from the country snapshots in `sources/manifest.json`, the repository's alias and self-continent policies, its continent display map, and any active reviewed local contributions. Taxonomy and research snapshots provide separate documented proof and are not additional country entities or direct build inputs. The evidence companion binds the complete applicable review records and their source links. Review records, approval digests and activation state accompany the package. Preserve the provenance companion when distributing a dataset.

Locally authored review text and metadata use this repository’s MIT license. Use short paraphrases in review records and link to the source; do not reproduce restricted documents. Referenced documents retain their own licenses. Linking to a source as evidence does not grant permission to redistribute the document, and its license does not automatically propagate to independently stated facts in Wikidata. We store reference links rather than copies of those documents.

Any additional imported dataset must record its source, version or revision, license, required notices, and the artifacts derived from it. Review compatibility before incorporation, and carry applicable attribution and redistribution requirements through generated outputs. A future dataset with different inputs must have its own complete license inventory.

## Consumer compatibility inventories

`sources/slackwater-required.json` and `sources/slackwater-source-audit.json` and the compatibility section of `reports/benchmark.json` record country input strings and counts from the pinned Slackwater revision, with unverified migration-baseline codes produced by `country-code-lookup` 0.2.1. It contains no station coordinates or prediction data. Slackwater's root MIT notice excludes `data/` and delegates data licensing to the individual source files; do not apply the root MIT license to imported source data. The complete pinned source and license inventory is recorded in [sources/slackwater-licenses.json](sources/slackwater-licenses.json), including exact per-license record counts, upstream credits, license URLs, source notes, and revision links. It covers all 8,728 records and 129 country inputs at revision `f9c3d42c066efe87e606f8c09673fb70cff2b2cf`; no station data is redistributed here. Preserve that companion and the upstream terms when sharing these inventories.

The inputs include DMI (207), JHOD (20), JMA (70), Kartverket (33), LINZ (56), NOAA (3,504), and TICON-4 (4,838) records. TICON contains 4,164 CC BY 4.0 records and 674 CC BY-NC 4.0 records; the latter retain upstream noncommercial restrictions. The inventory does not grant commercial-use permission or relicense those inputs. JHOD uses Government Standard Terms of Use 2.0; JMA selects the CC BY 4.0 option under Public Data License 1.0. Source credits and editing notices, including the TICON citation by Hart-Davis, Dettmering and Seitz (2025), are preserved in the companion.

These consumer inventories and their source-license inventory are excluded from the npm artifact by its `files` allowlist. The runtime country dataset derives from the CC0 Wikidata snapshots and any active reviewed local contributions; the consumer inventory is used only to check compatibility.

The `country-code-lookup` package is MIT licensed, copyright 2019 Richard Astbury; its notice is preserved in [sources/licenses/country-code-lookup-MIT.txt](sources/licenses/country-code-lookup-MIT.txt). `sources/accepted-inputs.json` is an unreviewed compatibility fixture derived from the CC0 Wikidata snapshots, local alias policy and any explicitly reviewed corrections, rather than a certification of those assertions.

The source audit carries consumer input/count provenance under the same source terms as the compatibility inventory. That inventory can select which Wikidata spellings to evaluate or admit; every admitted runtime spelling still comes from its separately pinned Wikidata snapshot. The audit and compatibility inventory are excluded from the package.

`sources/evidence-review-issues.json` is locally generated MIT-licensed tracking metadata containing request IDs and GitHub issue links, without imported issue prose. It records the exact candidate evidence digest and does not supply factual verification. The taxonomy and research manifests preserve hashes and permanent revision links for their CC0 Wikidata snapshots; research snapshots are not automatically admitted as country entities.

The candidate research log also contains locally authored summaries of NOAA station metadata and TICON-4 country identifiers. Their linked provider context retains the source terms and credits recorded in `sources/slackwater-licenses.json`, including Hart-Davis, Dettmering and Seitz (2025), TICON-4, https://doi.org/10.17882/109129, and any applicable noncommercial terms. Those query summaries are not runtime data inputs or copies of the provider datasets. Files under `sources/research/` and `sources/taxonomy/` remain separate proof; the research log can also cross-reference an already admitted country snapshot in the main manifest.
